Our Mission & Purpose

Restoring Dignity to Overlooked Communities

"To protect and empower vulnerable children and women in rural communities affected by natural disasters, insecurity, and poverty, through emergency relief, education, and sustainable livelihood support."

We believe that genuine change begins at the grassroots. Rather than one-off handouts, Nurova Foundation partners with local elders, teachers, and mothers to design interventions that foster long-term self-reliance.

🛡️ Child Protection 📚 Accessible Literacy 🌾 Agricultural Grants 💧 Clean Water Wells 🏥 Mobile Health Outreaches
Comprehensive Initiatives

What We Do

Click on any program below to discover our concrete purpose, aims, and measurable objectives.

Purpose

To stand with families in the first critical hours and days following natural disasters, sudden attacks, or forced displacement.

Core Aims
  • Deliver immediate food rations, potable clean water, temporary shelters, and hygiene packs.
  • Mobilize emergency trauma medical aid and critical wound stabilization.
Measurable Objectives
  • Rapid deployment to affected remote communities within 48 to 72 hours of an incident.
  • Provide 30-day survival packages to at least 500 displaced families during each crisis cycle.

Purpose

To ensure every vulnerable, orphaned, and displaced child is safely protected, nurtured, and provided high-quality schooling.

Core Aims
  • Dismantle the crushing financial hurdles that force children out of formal education.
  • Promote child rights advocacy and safe child protection frameworks in rural schools.
Measurable Objectives
  • Award 200+ annual full scholarships covering tuition, textbooks, uniforms, and daily lunch.
  • Renovate 5 dilapidated rural classrooms and furnish them with solar reading lamps and desks.
Support This Program →
Purpose

To equip widows, single mothers, and young girls with market-driven vocational skills and seed funding to achieve sustained independence.

Core Aims
  • Cultivate practical vocational expertise in sustainable tailoring, agro-processing, and trade.
  • Instill financial literacy, cooperative savings habits, and business management acumen.
Measurable Objectives
  • Train 300 women per year through intensive 8-week vocational cohort programs.
  • Disburse non-collateralized starter equipment kits and micro-grants to 150 top graduates.
Support This Program →
Purpose

To enable rural agricultural households to overcome recurrent drought, stabilize crop yields, and permanently escape subsistence poverty.

Core Aims
  • Distribute climate-resilient crop seeds, eco-friendly fertilizers, and modern storage bags.
  • Organize smallholder farmers into cooperative unions for wholesale pricing power.
Measurable Objectives
  • Support 120 smallholder family farms with high-yield grain seeds and organic inputs.
  • Construct 3 community grain-banking hubs to prevent post-harvest spoilage.
Support This Program →
Purpose

To deliver essential primary healthcare and eliminate cholera and waterborne infections through permanent solar-powered boreholes.

Core Aims
  • Drill dependable, deep-aquifer clean water boreholes in arid rural clusters.
  • Conduct free village medical missions focusing on maternal, newborn, and child wellness.
Measurable Objectives
  • Commission 8 solar-powered water pumps serving over 15,000 villagers every day.
  • Deliver deworming treatments, prenatal vitamins, and malaria bed nets to 1,200 mothers and infants.

Frequently Asked Questions

At Nurova Foundation, 94% of every donated naira goes directly to field programs (procuring emergency relief goods, paying school tuition, supplying medical outreach medicines, and drilling water boreholes). Only 6% is allocated to vital administrative logistics and monitoring. All disbursements comply strictly with the foundation's constitution and are independently audited.
